# COLO829 SV3 full RNA source screen

This audit extends the selected-site COLO829 RNA fixture with candidates from
all 115,269,780 spots of public SRA run `SRR8615617`. It tests whether the
observed SV3 RNA join connects to a versioned CNIH3 exon and reading frame.
The [machine-readable audit](sv3_full_sra_screen.json) records counts, tool
versions and the five exact junction-window spot IDs. The [small candidate
BAM](../../tests/data/colo829_whole_run/SRR8615617.cnih3_source_candidates.grch38.bam)
and [ten raw mates](../../tests/data/colo829_whole_run/SRR8615617.sv3_exact_window_spots.fastq.gz)
are pinned for repeatable report generation and tests.

## Source and retrieval

- RNA: [SRA `SRR8615617`](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra/SRR8615617) from the COLO829 matched-source atlas. Its downloaded
  aligned SRA archive required SRA Toolkit 3.4.1 to retrieve reference
  dependencies before FASTQ extraction.
- Genome: the pinned GRCh38 no-alt FASTA identified in
  [`sv_reference_flanks.json`](../../tests/data/hcc1395_sv_junction/sv_reference_flanks.json).
- Annotation: Ensembl 87 CNIH3 transcripts generated by
  `python -m reports.hcc1395_colo829.build_colo829_cnih3_models`.
- Initial RNA seed: the 1,213-base `88ab60e1f0f441a13cbecfe6` sequence in
  [`sv_transcript_evidence.fasta`](sv_transcript_evidence.fasta).

The screen target was `chr1:224430000-224745000` plus that initial RNA seed as
separate FASTA entries. `fastq-dump -Z --split-spot --skip-technical --readids`
streamed both ends of every SRA spot; no complete FASTQ was written to disk.
The stream was aligned with `minimap2 2.26-r1175 -ax sr -N 5`. Of 230,539,560
read segments, 1,677,693 primary alignments and 80,060 supplementary
alignments hit the small target. The target screen is sensitive to the seed
sequence but is not a uniqueness test against the rest of the genome.

Primary target hits were converted back to FASTQ with `samtools fastq -F 0x900`
and remapped to the full GRCh38 FASTA with the same minimap2 short-read preset.
SRA `.1`/`.2` suffixes were converted to one spot QNAME with first/last mate
flags and mate coordinates restored. The global remap yielded 1,273,581
primary mapped spots; 6,291 overlapped at least one Ensembl 87 CNIH3 exon,
of which 6,069 had MAPQ >= 20. All mapping qualities were retained in Isovar.

The original selected-site BAM was preserved for spots it already contained.
For 25,394 additional spots with a global CNIH3 placement, all their remapped
mate and supplementary/secondary records were added. The final candidate BAM
keeps all records for each of 26,976 spots with a CNIH3 gene-region placement,
including their off-target placements, and is coordinate sorted and indexed.
This is a *candidate retrieval fixture*, not the full transcriptome. A read
with no target alignment can still be absent. The SRA RNA and nominated DNA
calls come from different cell cultures or passages.

## What the screen found

The exact 43-base window is
`CTGTTGTCAGGC|CTATAGTAGAACTTCTTTT|GGAGTTGCTTCT`. Five distinct SRA spots
contain it in at least one read end, allowing either orientation. Three are
the original placed split-junction fragments. The other two have clipped or
partial genomic placements and add exact sequence evidence without qualifying
as placed breakpoint joins. Distinct spots need not be independent RNA
molecules; no UMI was available. These five are a lower bound from an
alignment-based target screen, not a certified exhaustive count.

The exon-anchored replay on the candidate BAM extends the joined context to
2,435 bases. It still has three placed split-junction fragments and zero exact
versioned CNIH3 exon anchor bases on the retained joined path. There is no
transferred reading frame or event-linked protein call. The assembly reached a
20-path limit, so alternative paths beyond that bound remain unevaluated.
Neither a single read covering 2,435 bases nor one covering the earlier
1,213-base path is needed to assemble a partial path. The 37-aa and 162-aa
ATG-to-stop sequences remain exploratory ORFs, not established proteoforms.
The missing mature path is tracked in [Isovar #563](https://github.com/openvax/isovar/issues/563).
Dense paired-template recount scaling is tracked in
[Isovar #562](https://github.com/openvax/isovar/issues/562).

Regenerate the two tables and assessment from the pinned indexed BAM and model
fixture with:

```bash
python -m reports.hcc1395_colo829.build_sv_transcript_evidence
```

That command also writes
[`sv_exon_path_evidence.tsv`](sv_exon_path_evidence.tsv) and
[`sv3_full_sra_extension.tsv`](sv3_full_sra_extension.tsv). The default
report replay does not download or process the 12 GB source archive.
